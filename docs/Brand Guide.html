Brand Guide · v1

Grow from the root up.

Rootly helps people care for plants with less guesswork and more confidence — turning care into a routine that feels natural and stays out of the way until needed.

WarmCapableNature-first CalmA little playful
The idea

A calm, helpful plant companion.

Rootly makes watering and care feel simple instead of fussy. It “gets” plants at the roots — hydration, growth, routines — without sounding clinical or overly cute. The brand lives in the sweet spot between friendly and capable: clean and modern, nature-first, with a subtle smart-home intelligence, and trustworthy enough to hand over your reminders.

Principles

What keeps Rootly, Rootly.

01
Calm over loud
Generous white space, one focal point per screen, one accent moment. We never shout, alarm, or clutter. Quiet confidence beats urgency.
02
Useful, then warm
Every screen answers “what does my plant need?” first. Warmth comes from tone and soft motifs — never at the cost of clarity.
03
Below the surface
We surface what’s usually invisible — moisture, growth, routine — and make it legible. Care that starts at the roots.
04
Out of the way
Rootly stays quiet until it matters, then nudges gently. Fewer, better notifications. The app earns trust by respecting attention.
Logo

The sprout & roots mark.

Leaves above, roots below the soil line — a single glyph for “care that starts beneath the surface.” Pair the mark with the lowercase rootly wordmark set in Bricolage Grotesque.

rootly

Clear space & don’ts

Do
  • Keep clear space of at least the leaf-height around the mark.
  • Use Canopy on light, Paper/white on dark, or single-color.
  • Let the mark carry warmth — pair with plenty of white space.
Don’t
  • Recolor the mark in off-brand hues or apply gradients to it.
  • Stretch, rotate, add shadows, or outline the wordmark.
  • Use the mark as an inline UI icon — it’s the logo only.
Color

A green core, warm paper, one fresh accent.

Botanical greens root the system. Warm paper neutrals give it air. A single honeyed accent — Sun — supplies warmth for exactly one moment per view. We deliberately avoid neon “eco-app” greens and cool grays.

Greens — the root

Warm neutrals

Accent — Sun & ink

Status

Usage ratio. Roughly 70% paper/neutral, 22% green, 8% everything else — with Sun reserved for a single CTA or “due” highlight. Status tints are for backgrounds; their solid twins are for dots, icons, and text.
Typography

Characterful display, humanist text.

Bricolage Grotesque brings friendly character to headlines and big numbers. Hanken Grotesk handles everything functional with warm legibility. DM Mono sets data — moisture %, day counts, growth.

Spacing & shape

A 4px grid and friendly radii.

Spacing scale

Corner radii

Default 14px for cards & inputs · 20–28px for hero/feature cards · pill for buttons & chips · circle only for avatars, the FAB, and status dots.
Elevation

Soft, warm-tinted shadows.

Elevation reads through blur and a hair of offset — never darkness. Shadows are tinted with ink, never pure black. On Paper, a card’s shadow alone separates it; add a 1px Stone border only on white-on-white.

Calm and gentle.

Behaviour

  • Fades with small translate/scale (≤8px, .96–1).
  • Press: scale to .97, shift to the deeper token.
  • Reminders ease in softly — never jarring.
  • No decorative spinners, no heavy bounce, no infinite loops.
Voice & tone

Gentle and useful.

A knowledgeable friend, never a lecturing app. Speak to the user as “you”; let Rootly act in the third person. Name the plant directly. Sentence case, low pressure, present tense. No emoji in product UI.

Do — say things like
  • “Your monstera is due for a drink.”
  • “Soil looks good for now.”
  • “Growth is steady this week.”
  • “Rootly noticed lower moisture.”
  • “Nice — that’s three weeks of steady care.”
Don’t
  • “ALERT: Hydration levels critical!”
  • “Water your plant NOW!!!”
  • “Optimize your plant’s H₂O intake.”
  • “Oopsie! Your planty-wanty is thirsty 🥺🌱”
Microcopy. Empty states invite (“No plants yet. Add your first to start tracking.”). Confirmations stay warm and brief (“Logged. Watered today.”). Time is plain language — “tomorrow”, “in 3 days”, “5 days ago” — not timestamps.
Iconography

Lucide line icons.

Clean, rounded, even-stroke line icons — recolored with currentColor at 20–24px, 1.75–2px stroke. Never mix filled and outline in one view; status dots are the only solid glyphs. Emoji and unicode symbols are not used as icons.

Substitution flag: Lucide is a deliberate brand match, not a supplied set — no proprietary icon library existed. Swap the reference if Rootly later commissions custom icons.
